Every window, United are "linked" with roughly half of the footballers on Earth. Here's how to stay calm.

## The rumour reliability scale

| Source | Reliability | What it actually means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trusted club-beat journalist | High | Something is genuinely happening |
| "Sources close to the player" | Medium | The agent wants a pay rise elsewhere |
| A paper you've never heard of | Low | Someone had a slow Tuesday |
| Your mate's cousin who "works at Carrington" | None | He works at a Carrington, technically |

## Five rules for staying sane

1. **Wait for the medical photos.** Nothing is real until there's a slightly awkward picture of a player holding a shirt.
2. **Ignore fees reported in other currencies.** They will be converted wrongly at least three times.
3. **Beware the "here we go" impersonators.** Check the account handle. Then check it again.
4. **Don't fall in love with YouTube compilations.** Everyone looks world class over a dramatic soundtrack.
5. **Remember the squad needs balance.** We cannot play eleven wingers. Probably.

## Deadline day protocol

Snacks. Phone charged. Expectations low. If something happens, celebrate. If nothing happens, remember the five stages and carry on.
