Psychologists talk about five stages of grief. United fans go through them every season, often inside a single month.

## 1. Denial

*"It's early days. The new signings need time to bed in. Look at the xG."*

The table says one thing; your heart says another. You find a stat that proves everything is fine and you post it everywhere.

## 2. Anger

The group chat goes nuclear. Someone blames the manager, someone blames the owners, and someone blames a full-back who hasn't played since September. Everyone is right, and no one is.

## 3. Bargaining

*"If we just win the next three, we're right back in it."*

You start checking other teams' fixtures. You become a temporary fan of whichever side is playing our rivals this weekend.

## 4. Depression

You close the app at half-time. You say "I'm not even watching the next one." You watch the next one.

## 5. Acceptance

Then a moment happens: a late winner, a debut goal from an academy kid, a performance that comes out of nowhere. You remember why you do this, and on the first day of pre-season you're back to stage one, full of hope.
