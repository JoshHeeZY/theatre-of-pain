There are nights you remember, and then there's 26 May 1999 at the Camp Nou.

For ninety minutes Bayern Munich were the better side. Mario Basler's early free kick had them ahead, they hit the woodwork twice, and the ribbons in their colours were already being tied to the trophy. Then came stoppage time.

## Ninety-one minutes

A corner. Peter Schmeichel up in the box. The ball breaks, Ryan Giggs scuffs a shot and **Teddy Sheringham** turns it in. One-all. Extra time beckons, and honestly most of us would have taken that.

## Ninety-three minutes

Another corner. Sheringham flicks it on, and **Ole Gunnar Solskjær** sticks out a boot and puts it in the roof of the net. The bench empties. Bayern's players lie on the turf. The treble is done.

> Football, bloody hell.

## Why it still matters

Because it's the reason we never switch off at 1–0 down. It's where "Fergie time" stopped being a joke and became an article of faith. And on the bad days, and there will be bad days, it's proof that this club can do the impossible in three minutes flat.

So the next time you're tempted to beat the traffic, don't. Barcelona says stay.
